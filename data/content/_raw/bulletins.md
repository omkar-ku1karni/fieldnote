# Service Bulletin SB-23-014: VRT-060 Condenser Fan Motor Run Capacitor — Early Failures
<!-- doc_id: SB-23-014 | date: 2023-11-06 -->

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin No. | SB-23-014 |
| Date | 2023-11-06 |
| Subject | Condenser fan motor run capacitor early failures |
| Applies To | VRT-060 units with serial numbers 2307V00001 through 2312V99999 |
| Category | Product improvement |

## Purpose

Provide field identification, corrective action, and warranty documentation instructions for condenser fan motor run capacitor deterioration on affected VRT-060 packaged rooftop units.

## Background

Original condenser fan run capacitor is rated 5 µF / 370 VAC and can swell and lose capacitance after one to two cooling seasons. A weak capacitor reduces condenser fan speed, which raises head pressure on hot days and can cause high-pressure switch trips.

## Identification

Locate the model and serial number on the unit rating plate. Confirm that the unit is a VRT-060 within the specified serial range. With the unit isolated from power, inspect the installed component for swelling, damaged terminals, or other visible deterioration.

> **WARNING:** Disconnect and lock out all electrical power before opening electrical compartments. Verify absence of voltage and safely discharge stored electrical energy using approved service practices before touching component connections.

## Procedure

1. Record the unit model, serial number, site, reported symptoms, and any observed protective shutdowns.
2. Isolate and lock out electrical power. Verify the circuit is de-energized before handling the component.
3. Inspect the condenser fan assembly for obstruction or mechanical damage, then inspect the existing run capacitor and its connections.
4. Test the isolated capacitor using a suitable instrument and compare the reading with its marked rating. Record the result.
5. Replace with upgraded capacitor part number VPN-0475 (5 µF / 440 VAC).
6. Secure the replacement in the existing mounting location, restore connections as documented for the unit, and check for loose or damaged wiring.
7. Reinstall all covers, restore power, and verify normal condenser fan start-up and steady operation during a cooling call. Investigate any remaining shutdown without changing refrigerant charge solely on the basis of this bulletin.

## Parts

Required replacement: VPN-0475, upgraded condenser fan run capacitor. Use the approved replacement identified above for affected units.

## Warranty and Documentation

Warranty labor allowance: 0.5 hours. Record the original component condition, measured result, replacement installed, and post-repair operating check. Submit the applicable warranty record. Contact Vantorr Technical Support if symptoms persist.

# Service Bulletin SB-24-003: VRL-150 Condenser Fan Blade Inspection
<!-- doc_id: SB-24-003 | date: 2024-03-18 -->

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin No. | SB-24-003 |
| Date | 2024-03-18 |
| Subject | Condenser fan blade hub inspection |
| Applies To | VRL-150 units built in 2023 |
| Category | Field service guidance |

## Purpose

Establish a repeatable spring start-up inspection for the condenser fan blades on applicable VRL-150 packaged rooftop units. Early detection reduces the risk of blade failure.

## Background

Cracks near the blade hub may be difficult to see while the fan assembly is installed or dirty. Continued operation can worsen a small defect.

## Identification

Confirm the VRL-150 model and 2023 build information from the unit rating plate or unit records. Check each accessible condenser fan blade during spring start-up and whenever unusual fan vibration or noise is reported.

> **WARNING:** Disconnect and lock out all electrical power. Verify that the fan has stopped completely before removing a guard or accessing the blade assembly. Keep personnel clear of the fan discharge area.

## Procedure

1. Document the unit identification and the reason for inspection.
2. Isolate electrical power, apply lockout procedures, and verify the rotating assembly is stationary.
3. Gain safe access to the condenser fan assembly according to the unit service instructions.
4. Clean accumulated debris from the hub and blade roots sufficiently to allow a visual inspection. Examine accessible surfaces for hairline cracks, distortion, or damage.
5. Inspect condenser fan blades for hairline cracks at the hub during every spring start-up.
6. Do not operate the unit with a cracked blade.
7. Replace cracked blades with fan blade kit VPN-6120; torque hub set screws to 160 in-lb.
8. Confirm blade seating and clearance. Reinstall guards and panels, restore power, and observe from a protected position. Stop for abnormal noise or vibration.

> **NOTE:** Do not attempt to weld, straighten, or otherwise repair a cracked blade.

## Parts

Fan blade kit VPN-6120 is the approved corrective replacement for cracked blades covered by this bulletin. No replacement is directed when inspection finds no damage.

## Warranty and Documentation

Record the unit identification, inspection date, blade condition, any replacement performed, and the final operating check. Attach photographs of suspected cracks when available. Process any claim under applicable warranty terms; no labor allowance is specified here. Refer unresolved fit or vibration concerns to Vantorr Technical Support.

# Service Bulletin SB-24-011: A2L Refrigerant Detection System — Field Service Guidance
<!-- doc_id: SB-24-011 | date: 2024-09-02 -->

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin No. | SB-24-011 |
| Date | 2024-09-02 |
| Subject | Refrigerant detection system and mitigation mode |
| Applies To | All R-454B units (VRT, VHP, VRL) |
| Category | Field service guidance |

## Purpose

Clarify expected refrigerant detection system behavior and provide a safe field response when a technician encounters mitigation mode on a Vantorr unit using R-454B.

## Background

When the refrigerant detection sensor senses a leak, the board shuts off the compressor and runs the indoor blower continuously (mitigation mode). Mitigation mode continues for at least 5 minutes after the sensor reading clears. Continued blower operation may be an intended protective response.

## Identification

Verify the unit series and R-454B designation from the rating plate. Consult the *Status LED Flash Codes* figure for diagnostic indications.

> **WARNING:** Follow site procedures for a suspected refrigerant release. Control ignition sources and use appropriate leak-detection equipment. Work only when the area is safe.

## Procedure

1. Record the unit model, serial number, location, reported sequence of events, and observed equipment behavior.
2. Identify whether the compressor is stopped and the indoor blower is operating continuously; do not defeat this protective response to obtain cooling or heating.
3. Never bypass or jumper the refrigerant detection sensor.
4. Assess the work area using approved safety practices and suitable detection equipment. Locate and correct any leak under applicable service procedures.
5. Visually inspect accessible sensor components, mounting, and harness condition only when safe to do so.
6. After the hazard is addressed and readings clear, allow the system to complete its protective operation.
7. Verify normal system operation only after safety conditions are satisfied. If mitigation persists or returns, stop further operating tests and contact Vantorr Technical Support.

> **NOTE:** Do not resume operation around a suspected leak.

## Parts

No routine replacement part is specified by this bulletin. Determine any required sensor or system component replacement from the applicable unit service documentation.

## Warranty and Documentation

Record observations, safety actions, leak investigation findings, corrective work, and final verification. Document any parts installed and submit warranty requests under the applicable terms. Do not claim a fixed labor allowance from this bulletin.
