# Service Bulletin SB-23-014: VRT-060 Condenser Fan Motor Run Capacitor — Early Failures
<!-- doc_id: SB-23-014 | date: 2023-11-06 -->

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Bulletin No. | SB-23-014 |
| Date | 2023-11-06 |
| Subject | Condenser fan motor run capacitor early failures |
| Applies To | VRT-060 units with serial numbers 2307V00001 through 2312V99999 |
| Category | Product improvement |

## Purpose

Provide field identification, corrective action, and warranty documentation instructions for condenser fan motor run capacitor deterioration on affected VRT-060 packaged rooftop units.

## Background

Original condenser fan run capacitor is rated 5 µF / 370 VAC and can swell and lose capacitance after one to two cooling seasons. A weak capacitor reduces condenser fan speed, which raises head pressure on hot days and can cause high-pressure switch trips.

## Identification

Locate the model and serial number on the unit rating plate. Confirm that the unit is a VRT-060 within the specified serial range. With the unit isolated from power, inspect the installed component for swelling, damaged terminals, or other visible deterioration.

> **WARNING:** Disconnect and lock out all electrical power before opening electrical compartments. Verify absence of voltage and safely discharge stored electrical energy using approved service practices before touching component connections.

## Procedure

1. Record the unit model, serial number, site, reported symptoms, and any observed protective shutdowns.
2. Isolate and lock out electrical power. Verify the circuit is de-energized before handling the component.
3. Inspect the condenser fan assembly for obstruction or mechanical damage, then inspect the existing run capacitor and its connections.
4. Test the isolated capacitor using a suitable instrument and compare the reading with its marked rating. Record the result.
5. Replace with upgraded capacitor part number VPN-0475 (5 µF / 440 VAC).
6. Secure the replacement in the existing mounting location, restore connections as documented for the unit, and check for loose or damaged wiring.
7. Reinstall all covers, restore power, and verify normal condenser fan start-up and steady operation during a cooling call. Investigate any remaining shutdown without changing refrigerant charge solely on the basis of this bulletin.

## Parts

Required replacement: VPN-0475, upgraded condenser fan run capacitor. Use the approved replacement identified above for affected units.

## Warranty and Documentation

Warranty labor allowance: 0.5 hours. Record the original component condition, measured result, replacement installed, and post-repair operating check. Submit the applicable warranty record. Contact Vantorr Technical Support if symptoms persist.
